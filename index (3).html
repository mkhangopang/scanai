Live scanning needs one setting on Android Chrome.
  1. Open chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features
  2. Set it to Enabled, then relaunch Chrome
  3. Open this page directly in Chrome (not inside another app), turn Bluetooth and Location on, then tap Start scan

Until then, Try demo shows how the app behaves.
